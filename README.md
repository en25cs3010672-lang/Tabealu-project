## 🏎️ Quick Tour: What You Can Test

### 1. Interactive Dashboard

Explore the dashboard's interactive features:

- **Season Filter:** Select a specific season, such as 2024, 2023, or 2022, or choose **All (2018–2024)** to update KPIs, charts, and standings.
- **Top N Parameter:** Switch between Top 5, Top 10, Top 15, Top 20, and All to control the number of ranked entries displayed.
- **DNF Reason Filter:** Investigate non-finish categories such as Power Unit / Engine, Racing Collision, and Gearbox / Transmission.
- **Rank Metric Selector:** Compare drivers by Points, Points Per Race (PPR), Wins, Podiums, or Reliability Percentage.
- **CSV Data Export:** Use the Export Data dropdown or the Export CSV controls in the header or leaderboard to download the current filtered data, where supported.
- **Interactive World Map and Bubbles:** Hover over countries and driver bubbles to explore additional information through tooltips or popovers.

### 2. Tableau Story — 7 Chapters

Open the **Tableau Story (7 Points)** tab to navigate through a guided narrative exploring Formula 1 data, from global participation to team championship performance.

The seven chapters provide a structured way to explore the dashboard's major analytical themes.

### 3. Data Model and Calculations

Open the **Data Model & Calculations** tab to review:

- Calculated field formulas
- The underlying star-schema design
- Relationships between the principal data entities
- The logic used for rankings and analytical metrics

### 4. Tableau Public Portal

Open the **Tableau Public Portal** tab to review publishing verification information and access the TWBX schema specification export, where available.
